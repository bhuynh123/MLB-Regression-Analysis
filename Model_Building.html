1. Step 1 - Model Specification

H9 - Hits per 9 innings - pitching Non-P - non-pitcher postions(fielding) - position HitAllow - Hits Allowed - pitching ERA - earned run average(how effective pitchers prevent runs) - pitching RA/G_P - runs allowed per game - pitching DefEff - defensive efficiency - fielding OPS+ - on base percentage + slugging percentage - batting R/G - runs scored per game - batting WHIP - walks + hits per inning pitched - pitching

I chose the above variables for my model. The reasoning is that they all had a correlation coefficient over +-0.7 with our predictor variable wins. When looking back at the EDA, they also seemed to all have a strong linear correlation with our predictor variables. Below are all the variables that also had a correlation coefficient of +-0.7 or more but were elimintated because of a reason.

R_P - Redundant Same as RA/G_D but divided by number of games, has a lower coefficient. ER - Redundant Same as ERA but total, not average earned runs, has a lower coefficient. OBP - Redundant on base percentage but not slugging percentage, has a lower coefficient.

Although I eliminated these for the first model they still potentially might provide value for the future but at the moment they are eliminated.
